The story behind Potato Wiz and what we're building.

Our Mission

PC gaming graphics settings are confusing. Most players either run everything on Ultra and wonder why their PC sounds like a jet engine, or set everything to Low and miss out on beautiful visuals entirely.

Potato Wiz was built to solve that problem. Tell us what PC you have and what game you want to play, and we'll tell you exactly how to configure it for the best possible experience — whether that's the smoothest framerate or the most beautiful visuals, or the perfect balance between both.
